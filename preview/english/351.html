Речення, у якому бачимо пропуск 58, має таке значення: «Водії повинні знати дорогу вперед і в зворотному напрямку, що є складним завданням у лабіринті лондонських вулиць з одностороннім рухом і закритими для руху пішохідними зонами» (Drivers must know their directions backwards and forwards, which is a (58) __________ task in the maze of London’s one-way streets and blocked-off pedestrian zones).

У реченні пропущено прикметник. Проаналізувавши надані варіанти відповідей (A complicating (ускладнюючий), B complicated (ускладнений, складний), C complicate (ускладнювати), D complication (ускладнення, складність)), можемо зазначити, що лише варіант B complicated є прикметником, що узгоджується з іменником task, а тому є єдиною правильною відповіддю.

You’ve read a book about a famous person. You’ve decided to share your impressions with a pen-friend. Write a letter to your English pen-friend in which you tell him/her

  • who the book is about and what this person is famous for
  • what area of activity you’d like to achieve success in and why
  • what personal characteristics one should have to succeed in life.

Write a letter of at least 100 words. Do not write your own name, any dates, addresses or other personal information. Start your letter in an appropriate way.

Зразок написання № 1

Dear Bob!

Hello! How are you doing? Hope, you’re fine. Thanks for the letter about your participation in the math contest. My congratulations on winning it! Sorry, I didn’t answer right away, but I have been very busy at school lately.

To start off, I want to tell you about the book that has greatly impressed me. My dad recommended me to read a book “Google” about Bill Gates. And now I understand that Bill Gates is a wonderful role model. A technologist, business leader, and philanthropist, Bill Gates founded and built the world’s largest software business, Microsoft. Gates wrote his first software program at 13 when he was a schoolboy.  Although his parents wanted him to become a lawyer, he chose another way and became famous. Gates and his wife formed the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation. Today, this is one of the largest charitable foundations in the world. Like most successful businessmen, Bill Gates’ success came from a combination of hard work, intelligence, business sense, and luck. Gates constantly challenged his employees to work harder and innovate, but he also worked as hard or even harder than the people who worked for him. Gates also wasn’t afraid to take risks. He took a risk when he dropped out of Harvard to start his own company. However, his risks were calculated. He had confidence in himself and his product. He is a good example to follow.

As for me, I’ve always been keen on foreign languages and hope to become a real professional in this field. To be more precise, I’ve been thinking about working in tourism, because in this case I’ll be able to use my knowledge of foreign languages and have an opportunity to travel all over the world.

I’d like to say that if you want to be successful in our competitive world nowadays you should be ambitious and very hardworking. You will never achieve success if you don’t strive for it, so it’s important to set yourself ambitious goals. Besides, you have, first, to study a lot and, then, to work hard to go up career ladders. I hope I have the necessary qualities.

Well, I must be finishing now. I promised to help my Dad. Write back soon. I’m looking forward to hearing from you.

Best wishes,
Tom
