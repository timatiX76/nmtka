The idea for the website came from their neighbour, film producer Gaylon Emerzian. Isabella, 11, says when Emerzian asked the sisters if they’d like to help with a cooking website for kids, they knew right away that it would be fun. “We could do a website ourselves at home and reach kids all around the world”, Isabella says. “We get recipes and fan mail from lots of different countries.”

The sisters film new segments every two weeks. They set aside four or five hours to work, often while the neighbourhood kids are outside playing, and have learned a lot about lighting, filming, and editing. “The sacrifice is worth it”, says Isabella. “Sometimes it is hard to fit in, but when you see your finished work on the website, it is the greatest feeling!”

Olivia, 9, has this advice: “If you mess up, it is just fine. I mess up all the time!” She says she’s short and often can’t reach bowls to stir properly. She’s learned to use a stool and keep trying, “Mess-ups can be frustrating, but you’ll get it some time!”

Since winning the award, the girls have been interviewed by television, radio, and newspaper reporters. They’ve learned that hard work can not only turn out to be fun, but can lead to surprising results. Isabella and Olivia have also learned that one good idea can lead to another. Their new cookbook will roll off the presses this fall.

Цей пропуск бачимо в підрядній частині умовного речення. Завдання перевіряє вживання дієслів у підрядних реченнях умови, введених сполучником if на позначення теперішнього або майбутнього часу: This case demonstrates the clear evidence captured by the camera and should serve as a reminder to other road users of the potential consequences if they (42) __________ to take unnecessary and dangerous risks.

Отже, у цьому випадку пропущеним є дієслово-присудок. Серед запропонованих варіантів тільки варіант A choose узгоджується в особі і числі з підметом підрядного речення they, а тому є правильною відповіддю (варіант B chooses не узгоджується в особі і числі з підметом умовного речення they, варіант C had chosen має форму минулого часу й не узгоджується з головною частиною речення, варіант D will choose є неприйнятним, оскільки вживання форм майбутнього часу в умовних реченнях не дозволяється).

Правильна відповідь – А.

Зразок написання власного висловлення

Hi Kate,

Thanks for your letter. It was interesting to read about your family and your weekends. As for me, I have an important piece of news, which will drive you crazy. My family and I, of course, have moved into a new house. And I want to share my impressions of our new house.

To tell you the truth, I didn’t want to leave my flat at first and my parents’ decision to move the house shocked me. But when we came to our new house I understood that it was a house of my dreams. It is a two-storeyed house with big windows and, what is very important, it’s situated not far from the city. There is a beautiful garden behind our house where you can see a summerhouse and a small artificial lake with ornamental fish. On the ground floor of the house there is a spacious modern kitchen, a bathroom and a comfortable living-room with a big TV. My room, my brother’s room and my parents’ bedroom are on the first floor. My room is big, light and cosy with two large windows. I like it very much.

What is more, next Sunday we’ll organize a house-warming party and I want to invite you to this party. My cousins Ann and Tom, our classmates John and Peter will come too. I hope you’ll be able to join us!  There will be many things to do. We can play badminton or table tennis in the garden, watch films or play computer games and, of course, taste some barbecue which my father will prepare. I’m sure we’ll have so much fun!

I think that is really easy to get to my new house. You can take a taxi or a number 5 bus which stops next to your house and goes to the village of Green. Our house is located at 8, Flower Street. I’m sure you won’t get lost.

I’m looking forward to hearing from you and I’ll be very happy to see you next Sunday. 

Hugs,
Kris
