Саме тому правильною відповіддю до цього завдання буде варіант В із зображенням рибки.

You have just returned from Britain where you attended lessons at Summerhill Language School The school arranged for you to stay with a local couple you hadn’t met before your visit. Write a letter to your host family. First thank them for having hosted you and describe

  • what family traditions you liked and why
  • what local food you enjoyed and why
  • what events or activities impressed you most

Write a letter of at least 100 words. Do not write your own name, any dates, addresses or other personal information. Start your letter in an appropriate way.

Розбір

Пояснення


Зразок написання власного висловлення

Dear Mr and Mrs Smith,

Hope you are doing fine. I’m writing this letter to say how grateful I am for your hosting me during my studies at Summerhill Language School.

I must admit, I felt very comfortable staying with you. I enjoyed so much your tradition of discussing at dinner everything that happened to each family member during the day. I think it can unite any family. Besides, I will never forget our day trips every Sunday.

As for the local food, I was especially impressed by the famous English apple pie with custard. Moreover, I realized my dream to taste fish and chips and I was not disappointed a bit.

Honestly, I was fascinated by many things there, but one of the most unforgettable ones was the trip to the amusement park in Brighton. Amazing roller coasters, horror chambers, delicious picnic – I liked it all so much.

Well, I have to end my letter now, because I promised to help mom with dinner.

I hope to have an opportunity to visit your wonderful country again and, of course, to spend some time with your family!

Best wishes,
Susan
